CRSP — CRISPR Therapeutics

  • 2026-11-08 — CRISPR Therapeutics reported Phase 1 data for zugo-cel in refractory systemic sclerosis, presented at ACR Convergence 2026: 83% (5 of 6) of patients receiving standard lymphodepletion achieved a clinical response (rCRISS-25) at Month 3, with all responders maintaining response, and the therapy was well tolerated in all 21 patients dosed to date across the rheumatology basket, with no cytokine release syndrome above Grade 2 and no neurotoxicity syndrome. Why it matters: the first clinical efficacy signal for zugo-cel outside oncology opens a large autoimmune market for the wholly-owned CAR-T program, though the data are from a small, single-arm cohort. GlobeNewswire via TradingView
  • 2026-11-09 — CRISPR Therapeutics presented Phase 1b data for CTX310 in severe hypertriglyceridemia in a late-breaking talk at the American Heart Association meeting: a single course cut circulating ANGPTL3 by a mean 65% and triglycerides by a mean 63% at Day 30 in 10 patients with a median baseline triglyceride level above 1,000 mg/dL, with the reductions maintained at 90 days in the seven patients followed that long, no treatment-related serious adverse events, and no acute pancreatitis; the FDA recently granted CTX310 RMAT designations for severe hypertriglyceridemia and elevated LDL cholesterol. Why it matters: the first efficacy readout for the in vivo program in its lead indication, with deep lipid reductions and a clean early safety profile, though the data are from a small, open-label, single-arm cohort. GlobeNewswire

Next: zugo-cel Phase 1/2 data in large B-cell lymphoma at the ASH annual meeting, December 12–15, 2026. Management presents at the Jefferies Global Healthcare Conference on November 17, 2026, at 10:00 AM GMT.
