CRSP — CRISPR Therapeutics

  • 2026-11-02 — CRISPR Therapeutics reported Q3 2026 results with Casgevy revenue of $99 million, up 30% quarter over quarter and nearly six times the year-earlier quarter, more than 750 patients having initiated treatment worldwide; its own total revenue was $12.67 million, net loss was $111.4 million, and cash and marketable securities totaled $2.28 billion at September 30. The FDA also granted CTX310 Regenerative Medicine Advanced Therapy (RMAT) designations for severe hypertriglyceridemia and elevated LDL cholesterol. Why it matters: Casgevy's accelerating commercial ramp is the core value driver, and the $2.28 billion cash pile funds the pipeline for at least 24 months by the company's own estimate; the RMAT designations speed the regulatory path for CTX310. Securities.io
  • 2026-11-04 — CRISPR Therapeutics announced that Phase 1/2 data for zugo-cel, its wholly-owned off-the-shelf CAR-T candidate, in relapsed/refractory large B-cell lymphoma will be presented as an oral presentation at the ASH annual meeting on December 13, 2026 (abstract 706), with the title citing dose-dependent efficacy in highly refractory disease. Why it matters: the December data readout is a key pipeline catalyst for the wholly-owned program, though the announcement itself contains no new results. CRISPR Therapeutics / GlobeNewswire
  • 2026-11-08 — CRISPR Therapeutics reported Phase 1 data for zugo-cel in refractory systemic sclerosis, presented at ACR Convergence 2026: 83% (5 of 6) of patients receiving standard lymphodepletion achieved a clinical response (rCRISS-25) at Month 3, with all responders maintaining response, and the therapy was well tolerated in all 21 patients dosed to date across the rheumatology basket, with no cytokine release syndrome above Grade 2 and no neurotoxicity syndrome. Why it matters: the first clinical efficacy signal for zugo-cel outside oncology opens a large autoimmune market for the wholly-owned CAR-T program, though the data are from a small, single-arm cohort. GlobeNewswire via TradingView
