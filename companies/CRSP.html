CRSP — CRISPR Therapeutics

  • 2026-11-17 — At the Jefferies Global Healthcare Conference, chief executive Sam Kulkarni said CTX310, zugo-cel and CTX611 could potentially enter late-stage trials within about a year, and that CRISPR has more than $2 billion on its balance sheet to fund multiple Phase III studies itself; CTX611 Phase 2 top-line data is expected by year-end, and zugo-cel showed early CRISS-25 responses in 6 of 7 scleroderma patients. Why it matters: it points to pipeline acceleration toward pivotal trials and self-funding capacity, the key value driver for a pre-profit biotech — though the late-stage timing is management's aspiration, not a filed trial start. MarketBeat, via TradingView

A November 19 recap of the Jefferies fireside clarified that no Phase III timeline has been set for CTX310, pending an FDA meeting under its RMAT designations, with severe hypertriglyceridemia the first focus. The two most recent material items fall just outside this window — Phase 1 data for zugo-cel in refractory systemic sclerosis on November 8 (83% clinical response at Month 3 in the standard-lymphodepletion group) and Phase 1b data for CTX310 in severe hypertriglyceridemia on November 9 (mean 65% ANGPTL3 and 63% triglyceride cuts at Day 30). Next: zugo-cel Phase 1/2 data in large B-cell lymphoma at the ASH annual meeting, oral presentation December 13, 2026 (Abstract 706); CTX611 Phase 2 top-line data is expected by year-end.
