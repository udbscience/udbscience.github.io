PLTR — Palantir

  • 2026-11-02 — Palantir reported Q3 2026 revenue of $2.313 billion, up 96% year over year and above the ~$2.18 billion consensus, with adjusted EPS of $0.44 versus $0.41 expected; US commercial revenue surged 148% to $984 million, passing US government revenue ($953 million) for the first time, and the company raised full-year 2026 revenue guidance to $8.530-$8.534 billion (about 91% growth) and Q4 guidance to $2.649-$2.653 billion. Why it matters: the print confirms accelerating growth and a mix shift toward commercial customers, and the raised full-year guide is the number the current valuation rests on — though US commercial contract value growth slowed sharply, which bears watching. MarketFlux
  • 2026-11-05 — The UK government will not trigger the break clause on Palantir's £330 million NHS Federated Data Platform contract, the Financial Times reported, with Health Secretary Yvette Cooper keeping the seven-year deal in place despite backbench pressure; ministers plan to give Palantir another two years and then retender the contract with a view to a British 'sovereign' replacement. Why it matters: preserves a £330 million revenue stream and removes a near-term contract cliff, though the planned retender in about two years caps the long-term value of the deal. Financial Times via Traders Union
  • 2026-11-05 — AmeriLife and Palantir announced an enterprise-wide agreement to deploy Foundry and the Artificial Intelligence Platform across AmeriLife's Health and Wealth distribution organizations, with Palantir-powered capabilities already in production at Crump Life Insurance Services after a pilot; financial terms were not disclosed. Why it matters: another commercial vertical win that puts Palantir software into production workflows, though no contract value was disclosed. Newswire

Westpac said November 5 it will not renew its Palantir contract (financial-markets compliance and cyber security); a Changshin footwear-manufacturing partnership was announced November 4.
