PLTR — Palantir

  • 2026-11-05 — The UK government will not trigger the break clause on Palantir's £330 million NHS Federated Data Platform contract, the Financial Times reported, with Health Secretary Yvette Cooper keeping the seven-year deal in place despite backbench pressure; ministers plan to give Palantir another two years and then retender the contract with a view to a British 'sovereign' replacement. Why it matters: preserves a £330 million revenue stream and removes a near-term contract cliff, though the planned retender in about two years caps the long-term value of the deal. Financial Times via Traders Union
  • 2026-11-06 — Palantir won a £10.1 million five-year contract with the combined Bedfordshire, Cambridgeshire and Hertfordshire police forces for its BCH Foundry Platform, running October 9, 2026 to October 8, 2031 — its largest known UK police contract — after the contract notice appeared on Contracts Finder on November 6. Why it matters: shows continued UK public-sector expansion beyond the NHS, though £10 million is small against Palantir's roughly $8.5 billion full-year revenue guide. PublicTechnology
  • 2026-11-09 — The Guardian reported, from messages released under freedom of information, that Palantir's UK chief lobbied a health minister to extend its NHS tools into GP patient records, which were expressly excluded from its £330 million contract, with ministers having promised in 2023 that GP data would not be part of the national platform. Why it matters: adds reputational and regulatory scrutiny around the NHS relationship just as the contract's future is being decided, though lobbying alone does not change the contract. The Guardian

Former Democratic Senator Jon Tester joined Palantir as an adviser on its government business on November 9 (advisory, not lobbying). Westpac said November 5 it will not renew its Palantir contract (financial-markets compliance and cyber security). AmeriLife announced an enterprise-wide Foundry and AIP agreement on November 5, with workflows already in production at Crump Life Insurance Services. The White House nominated Palantir chief information security officer Michael LoSapio to lead the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency on November 9; Senator Ron Wyden's office said the same week that compulsory processes are on the table if Palantir does not answer his questions next year as Finance Committee chairman. BMJ reported November 6 that the UK government will extend the NHS Federated Data Platform deal by two years while putting it out to competitive tender, with expressions of interest from January.
