WMT — Walmart

  • 2026-11-20 — CEO John Furner told CNBC’s Squawk Pod that diesel has been running “in the six range” per gallon versus a sub-$4 budget, and that diesel and transport cost increases “work their way in the cost of goods”; CFO John David Rainey said diesel added about $2 billion in costs this year, and price rollbacks fell from 11,000+ items in Q2 to about 7,700 in Q3. Why it matters: a direct cost threat to Walmart’s low-price edge and rollback strategy, even as guidance was raised — the offset so far has been tariff refunds and mix. CNBC Squawk Pod
  • 2026-11-25 — Flipkart announced group CEO Kalyan Krishnamurthy is concluding his tenure after more than eleven years with the company; his last day is December 4, chief product and technology officer Balaji Thiagarajan and the board will lead in the interim, and a successor will be named in the coming weeks — no reason for the departure was given, and reports link it to differences with Walmart over the IPO timeline. Why it matters: a leadership change at Walmart’s largest international e-commerce asset just as its long-delayed IPO is in question — the exit removes the executive who steered Flipkart since Walmart’s 2018 acquisition and leaves the listing’s timing and terms to a successor. Walmart
  • 2026-11-25 — Walmart-backed PhonePe revived its IPO plans and is targeting a listing in the first three months of 2027 at a $12–15 billion valuation, with updated draft papers to be filed with India’s SEBI within 30–45 days and a pure offer for sale of $1.2–1.5 billion; majority owner Walmart would trim its stake by about 12% while Tiger Global and Microsoft plan to exit fully. Why it matters: a $12–15 billion listing would crystallise value in Walmart’s Indian fintech holding — PhonePe processed nearly 11.4 billion UPI transactions in October — and a partial Walmart exit returns cash, though the valuation and timing still rest on unnamed sources and the updated filing.
