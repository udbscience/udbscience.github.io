WMT — Walmart

  • 2026-11-19 — Walmart reported Q3 fiscal 2027 results: revenue rose 5.2% to $188.8 billion, ahead of the $187.4 billion expected, adjusted EPS was $0.65 versus $0.64 expected, U.S. e-commerce sales rose 23% and global e-commerce 17%; the company raised its full-year outlook for a third time this year, to net sales growth of 4.9%–5.2% (from 4%–5%) and adjusted EPS of $2.84–$2.89 (from $2.80–$2.87), though the sales outlook remains below analysts’ 5.5% expectation. Why it matters: a beat-and-raise into the holiday quarter led by digital and membership economics, though U.S. comparable sales of 2.9% matched estimates and the raised sales guide still trails the Street. Reuters
  • 2026-11-20 — CEO John Furner told CNBC’s Squawk Pod that diesel has been running “in the six range” per gallon versus a sub-$4 budget, and that diesel and transport cost increases “work their way in the cost of goods”; CFO John David Rainey said diesel added about $2 billion in costs this year, and price rollbacks fell from 11,000+ items in Q2 to about 7,700 in Q3. Why it matters: a direct cost threat to Walmart’s low-price edge and rollback strategy, even as guidance was raised — the offset so far has been tariff refunds and mix. CNBC Squawk Pod
