DIS — Walt Disney

  • 2026-10-28 — Disney eliminated between 100 and 200 jobs in its Experiences division, including across Imagineering, its parks design arm, following a review of spending, investments and project schedules. Why it matters: trims costs in the segment that generated $36.2 billion of revenue and over 56% of operating income in fiscal 2025, though it raises execution questions for the parks expansion pipeline. TheWrap
  • 2026-10-30 — Disney named Carol Choi to succeed Luke Kang as president of its Asia Pacific operations, overseeing studios, Disney+, local content, consumer products, media networks and content sales across Greater China, Japan, South Korea, Southeast Asia, Australia and New Zealand. Why it matters: Asia Pacific is a key streaming and parks growth region, so new regional leadership ahead of Disney's November results puts execution there in focus. ad-hoc-news (citing Simply Wall St / Yahoo Finance)
  • 2026-10-31 — Puck reported that Disney has indefinitely shelved work on a long-rumored second theme park at Shanghai Disney Resort, with many of the eliminated Imagineering roles said to have worked on the unannounced 'Project Atlas' concept; Disney has not publicly confirmed the decision, while confirmed Shanghai investments (a Spider-Man land and the Enchanted Star Hotel opening December 16, 2026) continue. Why it matters: could redirect how Disney allocates its roughly $60 billion Experiences investment and affects the timeline for future large-scale park projects, but as an unconfirmed report it is not yet a company decision. Particle (summarizing Puck)
