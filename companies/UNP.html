UNP — Union Pacific

  • 2026-10-21 — Several agricultural groups, including the National Farmers Union, Farm Bureau, National Grain and Feed Association and The Fertilizer Institute, publicly opposed the proposed Union Pacific–Norfolk Southern merger, arguing it would concentrate about 50% of U.S. rail traffic in one company. Why it matters: shipper opposition is a direct regulatory risk to the merger's approval and terms RFD-TV
  • 2026-10-22 — Union Pacific reported Q3 2026 profit of $2.08 billion ($3.49 per share; $3.53 adjusted, vs. $3.47 expected), on revenue up 15% to $7.2 billion, driven by 6% volume growth and core pricing gains, and reaffirmed high-single-digit full-year EPS growth. Why it matters: volume-plus-pricing growth and a maintained outlook bear directly on standalone earnings power ahead of the proposed Norfolk Southern merger Morningstar / Dow Jones
  • 2026-10-22 — Norfolk Southern, Union Pacific's agreed merger partner, reported Q3 income from railway operations up 4.7% to $1.15 billion, adjusted EPS of $3.62 versus $3.46 expected, and revenue up 14% to $3.5 billion. Why it matters: the merger partner's standalone earnings power and demand trend bear on the value and terms of the proposed combination Morningstar / Dow Jones
