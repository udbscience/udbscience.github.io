AMZN — Amazon

  • 2026-11-17 — Blackstone Digital Infrastructure Trust agreed to buy a 75% stake in two Amazon Web Services joint ventures holding 13 newly built data halls with 647 MW of capacity across campuses in northern Indiana and Mississippi, in a deal valued at about $5 billion; AWS keeps 25% and will lease the portfolio back for an initial 12 years on a triple-net basis at a 7.0% capitalisation rate, with closing expected in Q4 2026. Why it matters: a major capital-recycling move that brings outside money into AWS's physical footprint as AI buildout costs mount — Amazon generated $168.3 billion of operating cash flow over the last twelve months but spent more on property and equipment, leaving a $42.4 billion free-cash-flow deficit — though AWS still bears the lease cost.   • 2026-11-19 — Anthropic priced its IPO at $83 a share, above the $70–$78 range, selling about 980 million shares and raising about $81.3 billion, and begins trading on Nasdaq as ANT today; at $83, Amazon’s roughly 14.7% stake would be worth about $158 billion by the filing math. Anthropic’s shares opened at $111.50 on Nov 19, 34% above the $83 price, and closed at $97.17, up 17.1% on the day. On Nov 20 the shares fell 8.5% to $88.91, with an intraday low of $83.05, just above the $83 IPO price. Why it matters: an above-range pricing lifts the marked value of Amazon’s Anthropic holding at first trade. Vanda Team
  • 2026-11-20 — President Donald Trump said at a White House briefing that the U.S. would impose a “tremendous tax” on American companies that build AI data centers overseas, naming Amazon, Google and Microsoft and singling out Google’s planned data center in Finland; no tax rate, enforcement date or legal mechanism was given, and no executive order or bill has been published. Why it matters: a presidential threat aimed squarely at the hyperscalers’ overseas AI buildout, including AWS’s international data-center plans, though with no mechanism it is a statement of intent, not policy yet.
