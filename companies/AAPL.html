AAPL — Apple

  • 2026-11-23 — Thirty-two states and the District of Columbia filed a friend-of-the-court brief at the U.S. Supreme Court urging the justices to reject Apple’s bid to overturn a 2025 contempt order over its App Store, arguing a ruling for Apple “would be a windfall for competition-law violators”; the justices hear the appeal on Jan 11, 2027. Why it matters: the states’ backing strengthens Epic’s position in the App Store antitrust fight that governs Apple’s services economics, though the contempt order itself does not change the App Store rules already in force. Reuters
  • 2026-11-26 — Apple held the top spot in China’s smartphone market during the Singles’ Day shopping festival despite an 8% year-over-year sales decline, while the overall market fell 17% across the promotional period (weeks 42–46, Oct 12–Nov 13); six of the top 10 bestsellers were iPhone 17 and 18 models, led by the iPhone 18 Pro Max, and Huawei was the only major brand to grow, up 29% — Counterpoint Research attributes the weak festival to buyers postponing upgrades rather than accepting higher prices. Why it matters: a demand read on Apple’s most closely watched market — Apple outpaced a sharply weaker category, but the 8% decline and price-driven upgrade delays cut against the iPhone 18 launch strength seen earlier in the autumn. MacRumors (Counterpoint)
  • 2026-11-27 — OpenAI countersued Apple in Northern California federal court, accusing it of stealing trade secrets and blocking its access to hardware suppliers through “unlawful, tortious, and unfair business practices” — alleging a rehired former employee sent more than 100 confidential OpenAI hardware files to personal accounts and that Apple pressured shared vendors not to work with OpenAI; Apple called the counterclaims “a transparent attempt to distract” from its own July lawsuit, in which OpenAI has already been ordered to stop using Apple’s confidential information. Why it matters: the trade-secrets fight is now two-way and reaches into the AI-device race — OpenAI’s first hardware device, reportedly due in 2027, would compete directly with the iPhone maker, though every claim on both sides remains an allegation and trial is set for July 19, 2027. Reuters
