BABA — Alibaba

  • 2026-11-16 — Alibaba released Qwen4-Max, its new flagship AI model — a 2.4-trillion-parameter mixture-of-experts with a 1-million-token context window and native vision — available on QwenCloud at $1.50 per million input tokens and $4.50 per million output tokens, priced below its predecessor Qwen3.8-Max. Why it matters: sharpens Alibaba Cloud's AI competitiveness and pricing edge ahead of the September-quarter results on November 19, where cloud growth is the key test. Alibaba Cloud
  • 2026-11-17 — The US Commerce Department's Bureau of Industry and Security issued an interim final rule (November 15) requiring licences for advanced-chip shipments to data centres in Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia and Brazil and curbing remote access to advanced compute by China-headquartered firms; Alibaba fell about 5% in Hong Kong on November 17, and reports note it previously rented Nvidia capacity in Malaysia via Singapore-based Megaspeed International. Why it matters: it directly threatens Alibaba Cloud/AI's workaround for obtaining Nvidia compute capacity, a core growth driver — though the rule's legal authority to regulate remote access is contested and the rule may still change before it is final. Benzinga, via TradingView
  • 2026-11-19 — Alibaba reported September-quarter (Q2 FY27) results before the open: revenue of RMB 271.52 billion ($40.7 billion), up 10% year over year and ahead of the RMB 269.81 billion LSEG consensus, with AI cloud and compute revenue of RMB 60.97 billion, up 53%, and recurring revenue from external cloud customers up 67%; non-GAAP net income rose 92% to RMB 19.9 billion and reported net income more than tripled on investment gains, but non-GAAP diluted EPS per ADS of RMB 8.10 missed the RMB 11.16 expected, and free cash flow was an outflow of RMB 34.47 billion, wider than the RMB 21.84 billion outflow a year earlier. Why it matters: cloud/AI is now the growth engine, but the EPS miss and widening cash outflow test whether the AI buildout is paying off — the core valuation question for the stock. Reuters

On the November 19 earnings call, chief executive Eddie Wu said mass production of the next-generation Zhenwu V900 AI chip (three times the performance of the M890) begins in the first quarter of 2027, with V900 supernode clusters at commercial scale in the second quarter of 2027 and the J900 in 2028; he also said Alibaba's long-term 20 GW data-centre capacity target may be revised downward amid changing global compute policy, while cloud revenue growth is guided at 30%–45% next year and back to 50% or more in 2028, and the calendar-2030 targets ($100 billion external cloud revenue, 20%+ margin) stand. Alibaba disclosed on November 10, outside this window, that the Hong Kong Stock Exchange has cleared it to spin off T-Head, its data-center chip unit, which confidentially filed for a Hong Kong IPO on September 11; no size or post-listing stake was set. The final Singles Day tally put total Double 11 online GMV up 4.8% year over year to RMB 1.78 trillion, sharply slower than 2025's +14.2%, with platform GMV (including Alibaba's Tmall, the largest single platform) up only 3.7% versus 12.3% last year, amid price wars and cautious consumers, according to Syntun's final figures (November 12). The US BIS interim rule takes effect November 19 (Federal Register publication), with data centres serving China-headquartered firms given 90 days, to mid-February 2027, to obtain licences; commercial recommender systems are exempt.
