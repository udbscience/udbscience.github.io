BABA — Alibaba

  • 2026-11-10 — Alibaba said the Hong Kong Stock Exchange has cleared it to spin off T-Head, its wholly owned data-center chip unit (designer of the Zhenwu V900 AI processor), which confidentially filed a Form A1 for a Hong Kong Main Board IPO on September 11; HKEX waived the rule giving Alibaba shareholders an assured entitlement to T-Head shares, T-Head is expected to remain a subsidiary, and the listing still needs Listing Committee and CSRC approvals, with no size or post-listing stake set (IFR reported a possible $2–3 billion raise, unconfirmed by Alibaba). Why it matters: a separate listing could unlock value for Alibaba's AI-chip assets and give T-Head independent capital, though size, structure and Alibaba's post-listing stake are undecided and there is no assurance the listing proceeds. AI Stock Wire
  • 2026-11-12 — The US Commerce Department is reportedly finalising a rule barring Chinese AI firms from remotely using advanced chips in overseas data centres, extending export controls to seven more countries including Malaysia, Singapore and Thailand, with an announcement possible in November; Reuters reported the same day that Chinese AI firms are rushing to lock in overseas Nvidia-powered capacity before the route closes, and Alibaba fell about 7% in Hong Kong on November 12. Why it matters: it directly threatens Alibaba Cloud/AI's workaround for obtaining Nvidia compute capacity, a core growth driver — though the rule is not finalised, still under interagency consultation, and Commerce's legal authority to regulate remote access is contested. MLQ (summarising The Information / Reuters)
  • 2026-11-12 — The final Singles Day tally put total Double 11 online GMV up 4.8% year over year to RMB 1.78 trillion, sharply slower than 2025's +14.2%, with platform GMV (including Alibaba's Tmall, the largest single platform) up only 3.7% versus 12.3% last year, amid price wars and cautious consumers, according to Syntun's final figures. Why it matters: confirms weakening Chinese consumer demand into Alibaba's biggest sales event, a soft read on Taobao/Tmall revenue ahead of the November 19 earnings report. Business of Fashion

Alibaba will report September-quarter results before the US open on November 19, 2026 (board meeting November 18; call 7:00 AM ET; consensus EPS about $1.39–$1.46, revenue about $40.4 billion per Zacks).
