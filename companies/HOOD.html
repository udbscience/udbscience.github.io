HOOD — Robinhood Markets

  • 2026-11-05 — Robinhood Chain reported total value locked above $1.07 billion and cumulative DEX trading volume above $120.46 billion within 125 days of mainnet launch, the fastest to $100 billion in Uniswap history, and Robinhood and Arbitrum set up a $1.8 million developer grant pool. Why it matters: rapid chain traction supports Robinhood's tokenization and crypto strategy, though the chain is still a small contributor to group revenue. Cointime
  • 2026-11-09 — Robinhood launched its IPO Access service in the United Kingdom, letting UK retail investors request shares in eligible US initial public offerings at the offer price before trading begins, with no commission; the service launched in the US in 2021 and has taken part in more than 50 IPOs, and Robinhood is working with a UK Public Offer Platform provider to run it. Why it matters: the first international expansion of IPO Access extends Robinhood's retail-distribution franchise to UK customers, though allocations are not guaranteed and no upcoming IPOs were named. The Block
  • 2026-11-09 — Robinhood sued Missouri's attorney general and gaming commission in federal court, arguing the Commodity Exchange Act preempts Missouri law as applied to sports-related event contracts, after a California court curbed Kalshi's sports contracts on tribal lands and Arkansas sued Kalshi; Robinhood routes its customers' sports event-contract trades to Kalshi, Rothera and Nadex. Why it matters: the outcome decides whether Robinhood can keep offering sports event contracts nationwide — a growth line now facing state-by-state legal challenges — though the filing is only the opening of the case and the court has not ruled.
