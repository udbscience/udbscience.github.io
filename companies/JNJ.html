JNJ — Johnson & Johnson

  • 2026-11-05 — Smith & Nephew confirmed that talks with Johnson & Johnson over a possible combination involving J&J's DePuy Synthes orthopaedics business have ended. Why it matters: closes a possible strategic combination/exit path for J&J's orthopaedics unit. Smith & Nephew
  • 2026-11-05 — The FDA granted accelerated approval on Nov 5 for RYBREVANT FASPRO as a single-agent treatment for adults with recurrent or metastatic head and neck squamous cell carcinoma after platinum chemotherapy and an anti-PD-(L)1 antibody, based on the OrigAMI-4 study's 42% overall response rate; continued approval depends on confirmatory benefit in the ongoing Phase 3 OrigAMI-5 trial. Why it matters: the first new option in a decade for this hard-to-treat cancer, J&J says, extending its amivantamab franchise beyond lung cancer, though the approval is accelerated and conditional. AD HOC NEWS (Reuters)
  • 2026-11-07 — Johnson & Johnson announced new patient-experience findings from the Phase 2 DAHLIAS study of nipocalimab in Sjögren's disease, presented at ACR Convergence 2026: mouth dryness was the symptom patients most wanted improved (96% reported it), and a 50% saliva-flow increase occurred in 33% of high-dose patients vs 16% on placebo; the findings informed the design of the ongoing Phase 3 DAFFODIL study. Why it matters: supports nipocalimab's path in Sjögren's, where it holds FDA Breakthrough Therapy and Fast Track designations, though Phase 3 results are still pending. Johnson & Johnson
