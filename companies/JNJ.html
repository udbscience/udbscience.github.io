JNJ — Johnson & Johnson

  • 2026-11-05 — The FDA granted accelerated approval on Nov 5 for RYBREVANT FASPRO as a single-agent treatment for adults with recurrent or metastatic head and neck squamous cell carcinoma after platinum chemotherapy and an anti-PD-(L)1 antibody, based on the OrigAMI-4 study's 42% overall response rate; continued approval depends on confirmatory benefit in the ongoing Phase 3 OrigAMI-5 trial. Why it matters: the first new option in a decade for this hard-to-treat cancer, J&J says, extending its amivantamab franchise beyond lung cancer, though the approval is accelerated and conditional. AD HOC NEWS (Reuters)
  • 2026-11-07 — Johnson & Johnson announced that ICOTYDE (icotrokinra) met the primary endpoint in the Phase 3 ICONIC-PsA 1 study in biologic-naïve adults with active psoriatic arthritis: 58% achieved an ACR20 response at Week 16 vs 41% on placebo, with no new safety signals; the data were presented at ACR Convergence 2026 and support a planned regulatory filing for the new indication. Why it matters: a second pipeline win in a week that could extend the oral IL-23 drug beyond psoriasis, though the psoriatic arthritis indication is not yet approved and no filing timeline was given. Johnson & Johnson
  • 2026-11-10 — Johnson & Johnson said CAPLYTA (lumateperone) met its primary and key secondary endpoints in a second pivotal Phase 3 study in bipolar mania, improving manic symptoms versus placebo as early as Day 3 with a safety profile consistent with prior studies; the two positive Phase 3 studies will support a planned FDA submission for the new indication. Why it matters: a second positive Phase 3 result that could extend CAPLYTA from bipolar depression into bipolar mania, though the FDA submission is still to come. Quantisnow (PRNewswire)
