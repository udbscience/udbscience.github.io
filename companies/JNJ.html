JNJ — Johnson & Johnson

  • 2026-11-16 — Johnson & Johnson announced a definitive agreement to acquire Ollin Biosciences for $2.2 billion upfront plus up to about $1.125 billion in regulatory and commercial milestone payments, adding OLN324, a Phase 3 bispecific antibody for wet age-related macular degeneration and diabetic macular edema; Blackstone Life Sciences will pay $750 million over multiple years to fully fund OLN324's Phase 3 development, and the deal is expected to close before the end of 2026. Why it matters: confirms the reported deal at the FT's terms with development costs largely offloaded to Blackstone, a sizeable bolt-on for J&J's ophthalmology pipeline, though OLN324 is still investigational. Johnson & Johnson
  • 2026-11-19 — Johnson & Johnson submitted a supplemental New Drug Application to the U.S. FDA seeking approval of CAPLYTA (lumateperone) for manic or mixed episodes associated with bipolar I disorder in adults, supported by two pivotal Phase 3 studies in which symptoms improved significantly versus placebo as early as Day 3 and through Week 3; approval would make bipolar mania the fifth approved use for CAPLYTA. Why it matters: a label expansion for a growing neuroscience franchise, though approval is not assured and the indication adds to an already-approved drug rather than a new launch. Johnson & Johnson
  • 2026-11-19 — Johnson & Johnson announced the first commercial installation of its OTTAVA Robotic Surgical System at Memorial Hermann-Texas Medical Center in Houston, the first U.S. hospital to use the system under its early-access commercial program, following FDA De Novo authorization in July 2026. Why it matters: the first commercial step for J&J’s next-generation soft-tissue surgical robot, a MedTech growth leg, though the rollout is limited to select customers and no sales or contract value was disclosed.
