GOOGL — Alphabet

  • 2026-11-19 — Google began a global rollout of a redesigned desktop Search homepage: the classic “Google Search” button is replaced by three Gemini shortcuts (create images, ask about files, brainstorm) and a collapsible sidebar shows recent AI Mode chats; “I’m Feeling Lucky” survives and core search results are unchanged. Why it matters: Google is putting Gemini and AI Mode at the front of its most-visited page, the clearest step yet in shifting Search toward conversational AI — with the attendant risk to the link-based ad model. Android Authority
  • 2026-11-20 — Bloomberg reported YouTube has reached an agreement in principle to pay about $3 billion to thousands of U.S. school districts to settle claims it designed an addictive platform harming students’ mental health; the deal includes additional teen product safeguards, is contingent on a majority of districts signing on, will likely take months to finalise, and needs approval from Judge Yvonne Gonzalez Rogers; it would be the largest-ever corporate settlement with U.S. school districts. Google declined to comment. Why it matters: a roughly $3 billion litigation overhang on YouTube is near resolution, with teen-safeguard product changes attached, though the deal is not final and the amount is small next to Alphabet. LA Post (Bloomberg)
  • 2026-11-24 — Waymo recalled nearly 4,500 robotaxis in the U.S. after 11 incidents since October 1 in which vehicles entered closed highway lanes in roadwork zones, including three on November 9, despite previous software modifications; the recall follows a National Highway Traffic Safety Administration probe opened Nov 9 into school-bus safety violations. Why it matters: a safety setback that adds uncertainty to Waymo’s rollout and path to profitability, though the direct financial impact on Alphabet is likely limited. TradersUnion (Reuters)
