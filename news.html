  • 2026-11-19 — Johnson & Johnson submitted a supplemental New Drug Application to the U.S. FDA seeking approval of CAPLYTA (lumateperone) for manic or mixed episodes associated with bipolar I disorder in adults, supported by two pivotal Phase 3 studies in which symptoms improved significantly versus placebo as early as Day 3 and through Week 3; approval would make bipolar mania the fifth approved use for CAPLYTA.   • 2026-11-20 — Johnson & Johnson announced late-breaking Phase 4 STAR study results for TREMFYA (guselkumab) in biologic-naïve adults with psoriatic arthritis and axial involvement: the drug met its primary and major secondary endpoints, with more than twice the reduction in MRI-assessed sacroiliac-joint inflammation versus placebo at Week 24 (SPARCC −7.1 and −5.2 vs −2.4) and no new safety signals. Why it matters: the first dedicated randomised trial of an IL-23 inhibitor in MRI-confirmed axial psoriatic arthritis, strengthening TREMFYA’s differentiation in a competitive immunology market, though it is not yet a label change or approval. Johnson & Johnson
