CRSP2026-11-09

CRISPR Therapeutics presented Phase 1b data for CTX310 in severe hypertriglyceridemia in a late-breaking talk at the American Heart Association meeting: a single course cut circulating ANGPTL3 by a mean 65% and triglycerides by a mean 63% at Day 30 in 10 patients with a median baseline triglyceride level above 1,000 mg/dL, with the reductions maintained at 90 days in the seven patients followed that long, no treatment-related serious adverse events, and no acute pancreatitis; the FDA recently granted CTX310 RMAT designations for severe hypertriglyceridemia and elevated LDL cholesterol.
