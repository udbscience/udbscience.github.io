JNJ2026-11-07

Johnson & Johnson announced new patient-experience findings from the Phase 2 DAHLIAS study of nipocalimab in Sjögren's disease, presented at ACR Convergence 2026: mouth dryness was the symptom patients most wanted improved (96% reported it), and a 50% saliva-flow increase occurred in 33% of high-dose patients vs 16% on placebo; the findings informed the design of the ongoing Phase 3 DAFFODIL study. JNJ page

CRSP2026-11-08

CRISPR Therapeutics reported Phase 1 data for zugo-cel in refractory systemic sclerosis, presented at ACR Convergence 2026: 83% (5 of 6) of patients receiving standard lymphodepletion achieved a clinical response (rCRISS-25) at Month 3, with all responders maintaining response, and the therapy was well tolerated in all 21 patients dosed to date across the rheumatology basket, with no cytokine release syndrome above Grade 2 and no neurotoxicity syndrome. CRSP page
